About Imaaife

Fashion is more than what we wear

Hi, I'm Tolu Ajagbe a fashion psychologist, creative director and founder of Imaaife. My work explores the relationship between clothing, identity and psychological wellbeing. I combine behavioural science, creative storytelling and design to help people understand how fashion shapes confidence, self-perception and everyday experiences. Through research, workshops, content and creative collaborations, I'm building a future where fashion becomes a tool for emotional wellbeing rather than comparison.
